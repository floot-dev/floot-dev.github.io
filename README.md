# Floot Notes

Floot is a connector you add to your AI assistant, and it publishes the app your conversation describes. Here is what that buys you and where it stops fitting.

**Read the full page:** https://floot-dev.github.io/

Floot is aimed at people who want a working product at the end of a chat and do not want to think about servers, logins or deploys. If you already write code and want files you can open in an editor, the hosted model will feel like a wall rather than a shortcut. The one caveat worth repeating: the marketing page sets out flat plans rather than per-token billing, but it lists no figures, so the pricing page is the only honest source.
